Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026 Product: QR Code Generator (Extension & Web Studio) Developer: 79team / rowkin

Core Privacy Commitment: We respect your fundamental right to privacy. This software runs 100% locally in your browser. We do NOT collect, track, transmit, monetize, or store any personal information, browsing history, or user data on external servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the QR Code Generator browser extension and its web companion tool (available at https://qrcode.rowkin.xyz). Our architectural philosophy is built around "Zero Cloud Reliance" — all barcode generation, aesthetic rendering, image recognition, and camera scanning happen strictly inside your browser's local sandbox without transferring any payload over the network.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, process, track, or share any of the following categories of data:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We never ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or device identifiers.
  • Browsing Activity: We do not log, monitor, or track the websites you visit, URLs you browse, or tabs you open.
  • QR Code Content: The text, URLs, WiFi credentials, vCards, or images you generate or scan are processed strictly inside volatile browser memory and are never uploaded.
  • Analytics & Telemetry: There are zero third-party tracking scripts, analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics), or behavioral trackers included.

3. Chrome Permissions Used and Why

The extension requests only minimal permissions strictly required to provide its advertised features:

Permission Purpose & Justification
contextMenus Adds handy right-click menu items ("Generate QR Code" and "Scan QR Code from Image") so you can convert selected text/links or scan images on any webpage.
activeTab Accessed strictly when you explicitly invoke the extension action or right-click menu to retrieve the active tab's URL for quick one-click code generation.
scripting Used strictly to inject the lightweight in-page floating configuration card upon user command.
storage Stores your aesthetic preferences (custom colors, padding settings) and local history (up to 50 entries) inside chrome.storage.local on your device. Never synced to cloud servers.

4. Camera & Image Recognition Privacy

When using camera scanner or image drop decoding:

  • Webcam Feed: Video streams are processed in real time using client-side JavaScript (jsQR). Video frames are never recorded or transmitted over the internet.
  • Image Decoding: Dragging, dropping, or pasting images analyzes pixels locally using HTML5 Canvas. The source image never leaves your machine.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Commercial Ads

Because we collect zero user data, we do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any information to third parties. Our extension is free of charge and contains no commercial advertising or promotional SDKs.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our product is safe for all audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Updates to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy for compliance or feature improvements, changes will be posted directly to this permanent URL with an updated effective date.

核心隐私承诺：我们深知数据隐私的重要性。本软件100% 在您的本地浏览器中离线安全运行。我们绝不收集、追踪、上传、出售或存储您的任何个人信息、网络浏览轨迹或二维码输入内容到外部服务器。

1. 概述与核心原则

本隐私政策适用于 QR Code Generator（专业二维码生成器与工坊） 浏览器扩展程序及在线网站（https://qrcode.rowkin.xyz）。我们的底层架构坚持“零云端依赖”：所有的二维码生成、样式渲染、图像识别与相机扫码均完全在客户端沙箱内存中运算，无需且绝不与任何远程服务器交换业务数据。

2. 我们明确不收集的数据

我们承诺绝不收集、追踪或共享以下任何数据：

  • 个人身份信息 (PII)：绝不索取或收集姓名、邮箱、手机号、IP 地址或设备硬件标识。
  • 网络浏览轨迹：绝不记录、监控或追踪您访问的任何网页、浏览历史或开启的标签页。
  • 二维码内容明细：您生成或扫描的任何网址、文本、WiFi 密码、名片信息或图片，均仅在客户端浏览器内存中瞬时计算，绝不上报。

3. 浏览器权限使用目的说明

本扩展仅申请实现核心功能所必需的最小化 Chrome 权限：

权限名称 使用目的与技术合规解释
contextMenus 在网页右键菜单中添加“生成二维码”与“识别二维码”快捷选项，方便用户一键呼出功能。
activeTab 仅在用户主动点击扩展图标或右键菜单时临时获取当前标签页的 URL，用于免去手动复制粘贴、即时生成当前页面的二维码。
scripting 仅在用户主动触发时，向当前页面安全注入悬浮快捷配置面板，提升操作交互便利度。
storage 仅用于在用户设备本地 chrome.storage.local 中保存个性化偏好（如前景色、背景色、留白边距）及最近的本地历史记录（最多50条），纯本地存储，绝不同步至外部云端。

4. 相机与图片解码隐私保护

当您使用摄像头扫码或拖拽解析图片时：

  • 摄像头实时流：视频流仅在浏览器本地由纯 JavaScript 算法库 jsQR 逐帧分析，绝不对视频进行录制或上传至任何服务器。
  • 图片解析：粘贴或拖拽的图片通过 HTML5 Canvas 在本地像素级解析，原始图片始终保存在您的个人计算机中。
