Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026 Product: QR Code Generator (Chrome Extension & Web Studio) Developer: 79team / rowkin

Core Promise: We respect your privacy. This extension runs 100% locally in your browser. We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal information, browsing history, or user data on external servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the QR Code Generator browser extension and associated web tool handle your data. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Our software is deliberately engineered to perform all barcode and QR code generation, styling, and scanning tasks on your device without communicating with any external servers.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, process, track, or share any of the following:

  • Personal Identity Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or contact information.
  • Browsing Activity: We do not log, monitor, or track websites you visit, URLs you browse, or tabs you open.
  • QR Code Content: The text, URLs, contact cards (vCards), WiFi passwords, or images you generate or scan are processed strictly inside your browser's memory and are never uploaded.
  • Analytics & Telemetry: We do not include third-party tracking scripts, analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics), or behavioral telemetry.

3. Permissions Used and Why

The extension requests minimal Chrome permissions necessary to deliver its core features. Here is the comprehensive breakdown:

Permission Purpose & Justification
contextMenus Allows you to right-click on text, links, images, or the page itself to quickly generate or scan a QR code via convenient menu items.
activeTab Only accessed when you explicitly click the extension action button or context menu to obtain the active tab's URL so you can generate its QR code without manual typing.
scripting Used strictly to inject the lightweight in-page quick configuration popup when triggered by the user.
storage Used only to save your aesthetic preferences (such as selected color scheme, padding settings) and local generation/scan history (up to 50 items) in chrome.storage.local on your device. Never synced to cloud.

4. Camera & Image Recognition Privacy

When using the camera scan or image decode features:

  • Camera Stream: Video frames are analyzed in real time using local WebRTC and the JavaScript library jsQR. No camera frames or video recordings are saved or transmitted to any server.
  • Image Decoding: Dragging, dropping, or pasting images into the decoder processes the image pixels locally using HTML5 Canvas. The original image remains on your machine.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

Because we do not collect any user data, we do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any information to third parties. There are no advertising networks or data monetization brokers involved in our extension.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our extension does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please rest assured no personal data is collected or stored.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or legal compliance. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date".

1. 概述与核心原则

本隐私政策说明了 QR Code Generator（专业二维码生成器与工坊） 浏览器扩展程序及相关网页工作台如何处理您的信息。我们坚信隐私是用户的基本权利。本工具的设计理念为“零云端依赖”：所有的二维码生成、样式渲染、图像识别与相机扫码均完全在您的本地设备内存中运算完成，无需且绝不与任何远程服务器交换业务数据。

2. 我们明确不收集的数据

我们绝不收集、追踪或共享以下任何数据：

  • 个人身份信息 (PII)：绝不索取或收集姓名、邮箱、手机号、IP 地址或联系方式。
  • 网络浏览轨迹：绝不记录、监控或追踪您访问的任何网页、浏览历史或开启的标签页。
  • 二维码内容明细：您生成或扫描的任何网址、文本、WiFi 密码、名片信息或图片，均仅在客户端浏览器内存中瞬时计算，绝不上报。
  • 数据统计与遥测追踪：绝无嵌入任何第三方追踪脚本、广告 SDK 或行为分析工具（如 Google Analytics）。

3. 浏览器权限使用目的说明

本扩展仅申请实现核心功能所必需的最小化 Chrome 权限：

权限名称 使用目的与技术合规解释
contextMenus 允许在网页右键菜单中添加“为当前选中文本/链接/图片生成或识别二维码”快捷选项，方便用户一键呼出功能。
activeTab 仅在用户主动点击扩展图标或右键菜单时临时获取当前标签页的 URL，用于免去手动复制粘贴、即时生成当前页面的二维码。
scripting 仅在用户主动触发时，向当前页面安全注入悬浮快捷配置面板，提升操作交互便利度。
storage 仅用于在用户设备本地 chrome.storage.local 中保存个性化偏好（如前景色、背景色、留白边距）及最近的本地历史记录（最多50条），纯本地存储，绝不同步至外部云端。

4. 相机与图片解码隐私保护

当您使用摄像头扫码或拖拽解析图片时：

  • 摄像头实时流：视频流仅在浏览器本地由纯 JavaScript 算法库 jsQR 逐帧分析，绝不对视频进行录制或上传至任何服务器。
  • 图片解析：粘贴或拖拽的图片通过 HTML5 Canvas 在本地像素级解析，原始图片始终保存在您的个人计算机中。
